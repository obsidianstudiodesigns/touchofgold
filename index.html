only for you

Most of what we make starts as a conversation. Here's how a custom piece comes together.

  1. Step 1

    Talk it through

    Visit us or send a WhatsApp with photos, a sketch or just an idea. We'll talk about stones, metal, budget and timing, and size your finger properly.

  2. Step 2

    See the design

    We draw the piece and show you exactly how it will sit. Nothing is made until you've approved the design and the quote.

  3. Step 3

    We make it by hand

    Our goldsmiths cast, shape and set your piece at the bench, then finish it with a mirror polish. Every stage is checked before it moves on.

  4. Step 4

    Wear it for life

    Collect it in store, fitted and cleaned. Come back any time for resizing, re-polishing or a claw check. We look after what we make.
